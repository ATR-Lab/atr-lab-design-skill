# Kent State compliance

Every Kent State University rule that binds the Advanced Telerobotics Research (ATR) Lab's marketing and design work, in one
place: palette, fonts, the university logo, restricted marks, unit logos, co-branding, trademarks and merch, editorial style,
social media, websites, accessibility law, email and letterhead, photo releases, approval gates and contacts. Each rule has a
short source URL. Everything was checked on kent.edu on 2026-09-28.

**Read this when** a piece will leave the lab (slides shown outside the lab, posters, flyers, web, social, email, print, merch,
signage, video), when you place the Kent State logo, when you order anything printed or branded, or when someone asks
"are we allowed to...?". For the ATR logos themselves, read `references/logo-system.md`.

## 1. How to read this file

Kent State's University Communications and Marketing (UCM) publishes brand rules in two layers. Each rule here carries a
source label, and the lab's own advice built on top of those rules is labeled Derived, Recommended or Lab convention:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CURRENT** | The brand site `kent.edu/brand/*` and current UCM, Web Team and Policy Register pages. **Wins in any conflict.** |
| **LEGACY** | The older Guide to Visual Standards (`kent.edu/ucm/<slug>`). It is still live but no longer linked from `/brand`. UCM's transition note says to use it where the new guide is silent, so its rules still apply there. Its font lists and hex values are out of date. |
| **ARCHIVED** | Removed from kent.edu and read from the Wayback Machine. |
| **Derived** | A conclusion drawn from KSU rules that KSU doesn't state in so many words (for example, that the ATR marks count as university trademarks). Treat it as binding until UCM or OGC says otherwise. |
| **Recommended** | Advice, not a rule: what the lab should do to stay safe where KSU's rules are unclear. |
| **Lab convention** (or lab practice) | The lab's own practice, where KSU says nothing. It is labeled so nobody mistakes it for a university rule. |

Quotes are short verbatim excerpts. Everything else is paraphrased. If kent.edu changes, the live page wins. Re-check before
you cite a rule to UCM.

## 4. Color

**Primary palette** (CURRENT, https://www.kent.edu/brand/swatches)

| KSU name | PMS | CMYK | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kent State blue | 281 | 100 72 0 38 | 0 57 118 | `#003976` |
| Kent State gold | 124 | 7 35 100 0 | KSU prints 235 171 32 | `#EFAB00` (lab standard) |

- "The primary color palette should always be used as a starting point for design."
- "These colors should be used at 100 percent opacity." They "can be used liberally on all pieces".
- About the gold's RGB: KSU prints RGB 235 171 32 next to the hex `#EFAB00` (which is 239 171 0). The kent.edu CSS and the lab's
  logo art both use `#EFAB00`, so the lab standardizes on it. The visual difference is negligible.

**Refined (secondary) palette** (CURRENT, same page): "should be used sparingly and only in a supporting manner to the primary
brand colors." KSU gives these no names or PMS numbers. The names below are the lab's own tokens. Use the **printed hex**,
not the color of the swatch chips on the page, which differ.

| Lab token | HEX | CMYK |
|---|---|---|
| sky | `#2C8ECD` | 76 33 0 0 |
| flash | `#FFD702` | 1 13 100 0 |
| midnight | `#00295F` | 100 72 0 55 |
| steel | `#96A0A5` | 48 31 30 0 |
| silver | `#B5B8B5` | 30 22 25 0 |

**Metallics for print:** Metallic Gold PMS 873, Metallic Blue PMS 8783, Gold Foil No. 817. No usage rules are published.

**Also binding** (the tint rule is a lab convention)
- The LEGACY logo pages still print old web colors (`0A0D6F` blue, `FFAB1B` gold). These are **stale**, so never use them.
- On the web, "The primary background color should be white", and text contrast must be at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text)
  (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/web-team/web-accessibility).
- **Lab convention:** build light UI tints from the lab's neutral tokens (ink, slate, mist, line), not from transparent navy or gold. The exception
  is a photo or video overlay: KSU's own hero-video spec uses "Kent State blue solid with 50 percent opacity"
  (https://www.kent.edu/brand/images).
- Gold on white and white on gold are both 2.0:1, which fails for text and thin lines.

Tokens, contrast pairs and the chart palette: `references/color.md` and `assets/tokens/`.

## 6. The Kent State logo

**Required on everything.** "The Kent State University logo is our official identifier and is to be used on all forms of visual
communications. The logo should be clearly and prominently displayed" (LEGACY,
https://www.kent.edu/ucm/kent-state-university-logo). For the lab, that means slides, posters, quad charts, flyers, designed
social graphics (announcements, events, recruiting, results) and the website.
- **Lab convention, not yet confirmed by UCM (§19):** three things don't carry it. The social profile avatar (KSU's 10
  Required Elements want the unit's own image there, §12), plain photo or video posts with no designed layout, and the
  supplied video-call virtual backgrounds. When a call represents the lab outside it, open with a title slide that carries the
  KSU logo.

**Versions** (CURRENT, https://www.kent.edu/brand/logos, EPS/JPG/PNG zips): Horizontal, Stacked, Regional Campus, University
Seal (Permissions Required), "K" Emblem and three Athletics sets. The lab uses the **Horizontal** or **Stacked** logo, or a
college-specific logo if UCM supplies one (§8).

**Clear space and size**
- Clear space is the height of the "K" in KENT on all sides. CURRENT: "A GOOD RULE OF THUMB IS TO USE A 'K' SPACE AROUND THE
  LOGO". LEGACY is stricter: exactly the K's size, and it "applies to the logo at all sizes."
- Minimum (LEGACY): "one inch (1") with the word UNIVERSITY at least one inch in length". Scale up for large pieces (the
  LEGACY page's example is an 18 x 24 in poster). UNIVERSITY is the binding part. In the Stacked logo it spans 99.6% of the
  width (measured on the skill's copy), so a Stacked logo exactly 1 in wide falls just short. **Use the Stacked logo at
  1.05 in wide or more**, and measure UNIVERSITY on the Horizontal file before you size it.
- KSU publishes no minimum in pixels. **Lab convention:** UNIVERSITY at least 96 px long on screens (1 in at 96 px/in), which
  means a Stacked logo at least 100 px wide. Go larger where possible, and use at least 120 px wide on a 1080 px social graphic.
- Keep it at least 1/4 in from the page edge, a gutter or a border (LEGACY, same page).

**Integrity** (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/kent-state-university-logo and
https://www.kent.edu/ucm/incorrect-usage-logos-and-marks): the logo "may not be altered or reconfigured", "must bear the
registered mark" and "may not be used as part of a headline or running text." The incorrect-usage page also rules out:
- resizing or moving its parts, or setting it at an angle
- locking a slogan or other element to it ("Do not lock up promotional slogans to the logo", "Do not add elements to the
  logo")
- unauthorized colors, drop shadows or other effects
- the four-color logo on clashing or dark backgrounds (use the reversed logo), or any logo on distracting backgrounds

**Color versions** (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/logo-variations)
- Two-color: sunburst and UNIVERSITY in gold, KENT STATE in blue. Also Four Color, All Blue, All Black, Metallic gold
  PMS 873, Metallic blue PMS 8783 and Gold foil No. 817.
- Reversed: All White Reverse, or Two-Color Reverse (white with a gold sunburst). "The logo must be reversed from 100
  percent of the foreground color." White or ghosted versions go only on backgrounds "that are not complicated or busy."

**Placement** (LEGACY): "Preferred placement for the logo is at the top right corner of a publication page or the bottom right
corner of a brochure cover." KSU states no rule for slides or posters. UCM's own letterhead and PowerPoint preview center
the logo, so centered is also acceptable. Whatever the position, the busy-background rules above apply, so the logo never
sits on the lab's lattice, line-art or hazard band. Several of the lab's backgrounds have line-art in a right-hand corner. The
lab's conventions per format, with measured clear positions on each supplied background, are in
`references/logo-system.md` §8 and §9.

**Artwork** (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/miscellaneous-communications): "Only use print-quality electronic art."
- Download it from https://www.kent.edu/brand/logos. College and school logos come from UCM
  (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/how-obtain-logos).
- The skill's `assets/logos/ksu/ksu-wordmark-color.png` and `ksu-wordmark-white.png` are the Stacked logo extracted from an old
  lab deck: 1022 x 976 px rasters (the color file has been corrected to #003976 and #EFAB00, but both are still the old
  raster, not vector art). Use them for drafts and on-screen internal decks only. Anything public or printed gets the
  official vector files.

## 8. Unit logos and the ATR mark: the gray zone

What KSU says (all LEGACY, still binding where `/brand` is silent):
- Colleges and schools: "Official university logos have been created for all colleges and schools." They "must use only
  standard, approved Kent State University logos". https://www.kent.edu/ucm/graphic-identity-colleges-and-schools
- Programs: "The university does not have program-specific logos." "A program may not create a version of the Kent State logo
  with the program name below it." "Programs may not create separate program-specific logos." "Programs should use the Kent
  State University college-specific logo to which their programs belong." (same page)
- Departments: "Departments may use an approved department specific logo only." Official ones come from UCM.
  https://www.kent.edu/ucm/miscellaneous-communications
- Regional campuses and divisions: "Do not construct your own logo or alter the logo in any way."
  https://www.kent.edu/ucm/regional-campus-logos
- **Research labs are not mentioned anywhere**: not on the brand site, in the legacy guide, the style guide or the centers
  pages.

**Two readings, and the lab plans for the stricter one**
- *Reading A (unaddressed):* the ATR mark isn't a version of the KSU logo and reuses none of its parts, and labs aren't named
  in the rules, so the mark sits in a gap.
- *Reading B (covered):* a lab is part of its department, and a department may use "an approved department specific logo
  only". Under this reading, an ATR mark that UCM hasn't approved is non-compliant.

**Recommendation: get UCM review of the ATR mark, the roundel and the co-branding layout before any public use next to Kent
State identity.** That covers slides shown outside the lab, posters, web, social, print, merch and signage, not just large
campaigns. When you contact UCM (§18):
1. Ask whether research labs may keep their own marks and whether UCM wants to approve the ATR mark and roundel.
2. Ask for the **College of Sciences and Humanities** logo and any Department of Computer Science logo. KSU says college logos
   exist, and programs "should use" theirs. Until UCM supplies one, the academic Horizontal or Stacked logo is the fallback.
3. Show them `references/logo-system.md` §9 (the separate-signature layout).

**Until then, keep the ATR mark defensible**
- It never reuses KSU elements: no sunburst, no KSU letterforms, no seal.
- The lab name is never set under the KSU logo, and the ATR mark never touches it.
- "Department of Computer Science, Kent State University" appears only as plain descriptive type.

## 9. Co-branding

- **Two separate signatures.** The ATR logo and the KSU logo are never merged: no shared divider rule, no ATR mark inside the
  KSU clear space, no lab or department name typeset under the KSU logo, and no slogans attached to it. This follows from the
  program-logo rule, the integrity rules and the ban on locking elements to the logo (§6, §8). The divider files in
  `assets/logos/cobrand/` are internal previews only.
- **Placement (lab convention built on KSU's rules):** ATR left, top-left or bottom-left. KSU right: top-right on document
  pages and plain poster headers (KSU's publication-page rule), bottom-right on covers and slides (its brochure-cover rule), or
  centered. The chosen spot must be plain. Where that corner has pattern, lattice or line-art, use the nearest plain right-hand
  or centered position (on the lab's navy title background, for example, the bottom of the clear left zone). With partner
  logos, the KSU logo moves to the lower right. Details, measured positions and worked examples are in
  `references/logo-system.md` §9.
- **Hierarchy:** the KSU logo must be present and prominent, at its minimum size or larger, in an approved color version. On
  lab-authored pieces the ATR logo may lead visually. KSU sets no size ratio between a unit mark and its logo.
- **Partners:** "the Kent State logo must appear in the lower right portion of the layout with the partner logo/logos appearing
  in the lower left" (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/affiliation-representation).
- **No endorsements:** "Do not use Kent State LOGOS for ENDORSEMENTS" (CURRENT,
  https://www.kent.edu/ucm/social/guide-social-media). Any use of KSU marks that suggests the university sponsors, partners with
  or endorses a good or service needs a trademark license (§10).
- **Sponsors on the web:** sponsor references "must appear in the footer of the sponsored web page" and "must not exceed more than
  15 percent of the web page" (CURRENT, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/web-team/external-site-requirements).
- Partner-logo mechanics (permissions, logo walls, sizing): `references/logo-system.md` §10.

## 10. Trademarks, merchandise and vendors

**A license is required.** Kent State's trademark-licensing page (ARCHIVED 2026-03-06; its substance matches the live UCM pages)
covers "University trademarks, including word marks, design marks, logos, or symbol". It requires a license for "any Kent
State University trademark on or in association with any good or service", including "The production of merchandise for any
commercial or non-commercial purpose including the sale, giveaway, or internal use", and uses that suggest the university
sponsors, partners with or endorses something.

**Treat the ATR marks as university trademarks** (Derived). The Office of General Counsel's definition: "If you are using a
distinctive word, phrase, logo or other graphic symbol to identify and distinguish a university good or service, chances are
you are using a university trademark" (CURRENT, https://www.kent.edu/kent/news/legal-brief-university-trademarks). The ATR
mark, lockups and roundel identify a university unit's work, which fits that definition. OGC hasn't confirmed it for these
marks, so this is a working assumption (§19). The **roundel**, the `horizontal` lockup and `stacked-ksu` also read "Kent
State University", a
registered KSU trademark, so merch with any of them needs a license under any reading.

**What to do for any branded item** (shirts, stickers, banners with lab branding, giveaways, internal swag):
1. Get **UCM approval**. Internal units go to UCM (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/merchandising-and-promotional-items).
2. Order from an **Affinity Licensing**-licensed vendor (Kent State's licensing agent; the vendor list is on Affinity's
   Collegiate Clients page).
3. For promotional items, use a **KSU contracted vendor**: The Sourcing Group / AG Print Promo Solutions, or Consolidus
   (theKSUshop.com).
4. For print jobs at Kent State locations in Ohio, use a **contracted printer**. The vendors page FAQ asks whether a
   non-contracted printer can be used and answers "No". Large-format printing and installation go to Central Graphics,
   Arc/Riot or Scherba. Event lighting, audio and video go to Hughie's Event Productions or NPI Audio Visual Solutions
   (CURRENT, https://www.kent.edu/brand/vendors).
5. **Paid media:** "Small and/or local media purchases can be handled independently. All other media buys go through Fahlgren
   Mortine" (same page), via UCM.

**Trademark notices**
- "All official marks and logos are registered trademarks owned by Kent State University" (LEGACY,
  https://www.kent.edu/ucm/ownership-kent-state-university-marks-and-logos).
- "the '®' symbol cannot be used on trademarks that have not been federally registered". Unregistered marks take "TM" (goods)
  or "SM" (services) (Legal Brief). The ATR mark is most likely unregistered, so **never put ® on it**. Add ™ only if OGC
  confirms ownership and asks for a notice.
- **Trademark line** for printed pieces that carry KSU marks, "in small font size, preferably on the back of the publication":
  > Kent State University, Kent State and KSU are registered trademarks and may not be used without permission.

  (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/ownership-kent-state-university-marks-and-logos and
  https://www.kent.edu/ucm/miscellaneous-communications)
- **Recruiting pieces:** the LEGACY ownership page also has an equal-opportunity sentence for recruitment materials. It may be
  out of date, so **don't paste it**. Get the current wording from UCM or OGC.
- **Ohio Senate Bill 1:** recruiting, camp-scholarship and sponsor copy describes the work and the requirements, not
  demographic eligibility (https://www.kent.edu/senate-bill-1-compliance).

Production specs and the merch workflow: `references/print-and-merch.md`.

## 11. Editorial style

Kent State's editorial style "closely follows the Associated Press Stylebook", with KSU exceptions
(https://www.kent.edu/ucm/guide-university-style). The ones that bind lab copy most often:

- **University name:** "Kent State University" on first reference, then "Kent State" or "the university". Avoid "KSU" in
  running copy; it is for tabular copy or Northeast Ohio audiences only (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/n).
- **Units:** "University style is not to use acronyms or abbreviations for names of Kent State campuses, divisions, colleges,
  schools, departments, centers or programs" (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/n). KSU applies this even to research centers ("Do not use
  RCET"). So: "Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab" first, then **"the lab"** in running copy in every medium. "ATR Lab" is a
  brand name for logos, handles and display type. On a webpage only, after the full name, "ATR" may be used in headlines and
  links where space is tight (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/b).
- **No Oxford comma** (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/c-d). **"and", not "&"**, in unit names and body copy. On the web, "&" is
  allowed only in titles and links, for brevity.
- **Dates and times:** "Sept. 23", "January 2027", "9 a.m.-noon". **Phones:** 330-672-xxxx with hyphens. **Percent:** 5%.
  No superscript ordinals. "email", "advisor", "first-year student".
- **Titles after names, lowercase:** "Jong-Hoon Kim, associate professor of computer science". Verify titles in the catalog

## 14. Accessibility law

- **Policy 4-16**, effective May 1, 2017
  (https://www.kent.edu/policyreg/university-policy-regarding-electronic-and-information-technology-accessibility): all
  electronic and information technology "shall comply with Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973" and Title II of the
  ADA. It covers "all staff, faculty, and third parties providing EIT to or on behalf of the university."
- **Standard:** "All sites available to the public ... must be compliant with WCAG 2.1" (CURRENT,
  https://www.kent.edu/ucm/web-team/web-accessibility). KSU doesn't name a level, but the DOJ ADA Title II rule it refers to
  requires **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**.
- **Deadline: April 26, 2027.** This is the date on KSU's page, and it matches the DOJ interim final rule of April 20, 2026
  (https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/20/2026-07663/). A documents panel on the same KSU page still says April
  24, 2026, which is stale.
- **KSU's practical rules** (same page):
  - meaningful alt text; no text in images unless necessary
  - pausable animation, no autoplay over 5 s, no blinking
  - descriptive links (no "click here")
  - contrast 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; never color alone
  - a table of contents on long pages
  - accessible PDFs, and web pages rather than PDFs where possible
  - posted PowerPoint files are "particularly problematic", so convert them or supply an accessible version
  - captions on all video (review auto-captions)
  - text equivalents for org charts
- **Federal sponsors:** Section 508 (WCAG 2.0 A/AA) binds federal agencies. It can reach lab deliverables through contract or
  award terms, for example ICT delivered to NASA, DoD or NSF, so check the award terms. Meeting WCAG 2.1 AA covers it. No KSU
  text makes Section 508 itself the university's standard.
- **Help:** the Digital Accessibility team (https://www.kent.edu/digitalaccessibility) and EqualAccess@kent.edu.
- **The lab's own floor is WCAG 2.2 AA**, stricter than the law. See `references/accessibility.md`.

## 16. Photos, video and releases

- **Releases** (LEGACY, https://www.kent.edu/ucm/photography-and-videography):
  - Ohio law exempts the university's use of current or former students', faculty's and staff's likeness "for educational
    purposes or for the promotion of the institution of higher education" (ORC 2741.09(A)(5),
    https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2741.09).
  - "Individuals who are not employees, faculty or students of the university must sign a model release form". That includes
    visitors, partners, K-12 teachers and family members.
  - Minors get "special consideration and limitations". Policy 5-19 requires the parent or guardian to "execute all relevant
    forms and releases" for programs involving minors. **Lab practice:** a signed parent or guardian photo/video release for
    every minor before any image of them is used, and minors are never named.
  - "Special consideration" also applies to international students who are culturally averse to photography. Ask first.
- **Student names:** "Photos should not include student names without their written permission"
  (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/web-team/images-graphics-and-video).
- **Style:** "use available natural light when possible, demonstrate purpose and engagement and avoid staging photos"
  (https://www.kent.edu/brand/images). Prefer candid action to posed shots.
- **UCM archive:** PhotoShelter (https://kentstate.photoshelter.com/index). Archive photos are KSU property, and reproducing
  them without written permission is prohibited.
- **Video** (LEGACY, same page): no unlicensed music, trademarks, footage or photos. Shoot landscape unless the display is
  portrait. KSU branding is recommended. UCM can supply branded lower thirds on request. Captions are mandatory (§14).
- **AI imagery** is a lab rule, not a KSU one: never present it as real lab photos, robots or results. See
  `references/ai-image-pipeline.md` and `references/imagery.md`.
