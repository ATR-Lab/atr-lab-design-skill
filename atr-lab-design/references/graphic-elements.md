# Graphic elements, layout grids and motion

The ATR Lab's graphic language comes from the geometry of its mark: straight strokes, 45° angles, sharp
mitred corners and a triangle roof. This file covers the devices built from that geometry (the hazard band,
triangle lattice, chevrons, blueprint grid, the mark as a supergraphic and the chamfered corner), which file to use
for each job, the layout grids and spacing for every format, the corner policy, motion and what never to do.

**Read this when** you place a pattern, band or background; set up a page, slide, poster or social grid; choose
corner shapes for panels and cards; animate slides, video or web; or check a layout for off-brand decoration.

## 1. The geometry system

Measured on `assets/logos/png/atr-mark-navy-3000.png`:

| Part of the mark | Measured geometry | What it gives the system |
|---|---|---|
| Roof | Isosceles right triangle (45°/45°/90°): both sides rise at exactly **45°**, apex 90°. Its height is the clear-space unit X, see references/logo-system.md | The 45° diagonal: hazard stripes, chevrons, chamfers, wipes |
| Gripper arms | Straight bars at **45°** with **90° mitred elbows**; bar thickness about **6 %** of the mark height | The chevron `^` device, sharp mitre joins, the "bold" stroke weight |
| Letter cut-outs in the roof | Straight cuts at 0°, 90° and 45° | Straight cuts only, no curves |
| Spike (the "T") | Two edges about 10° off vertical, meeting in a sharp point | Belongs to the mark only; do not reuse this angle elsewhere |
| Curves | None | No rounded, organic or hand-drawn shapes in brand devices |

**Primary angle 45°** (with 0° and 90°). **Secondary angle 60°**: the equilateral triangle lattice and the hexagons
in the icon set. The 60° family is a sibling of the roof, not a copy of it; it adds a "triangle" texture that
reads as engineering without repeating the mark. Nothing else: no free angles such as 15°, 20° or 75°.

Construction rules that follow from this:

- **Joins are mitred (sharp), caps are butt.** No round joins or caps in patterns, chevrons, rules or diagrams.
- **Two stroke weights for decoration.** *Hairline* for texture (the lattice is 1.5 px on a 1920 px artboard,
  about 0.08 % of the width; 0.5-0.75 pt in print) and *bold* for accents (the chevron is 6 px at 1920; icon
  strokes are about 8 % of the glyph width). Nothing in between, so texture never competes with content.
  (Diagram connectors and chart lines are content, not decoration: see references/data-visualization.md.)
- **One colour per device.** A band is gold plus its ground; a lattice is one line colour on one field.
- **Tokens only:** navy `#003976`, gold `#EFAB00`, midnight `#00295F`, sky `#2C8ECD`, mist `#F3F6FA`,
  line `#D6DEE8`, white. See references/color.md.

## 3. Hazard-stripe band

The lab's signature: diagonal gold stripes, measured from the lab's original section slide. It says "robotics lab,
safety first" and ties the slides, signage and events together. Because it is loud, it is used deliberately:
dividers, title and closing edges, event and safety signage, one edge of a poster. Not every slide.

**Construction** (everything scales with the band height *h*):

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Stripe angle | 45°, rising to the right (`/`) |
| Stripe width, measured perpendicular to the stripe | w = 2h (measured 2.04 in the original); gold and ground are 1:1 |
| Horizontal period | P = 2·w·√2 = 4√2·h ≈ 5.657h (a 96 px band repeats every 543.06 px) |
| One stripe (parallelogram) | (x₀, 0) → (x₀ + w√2, 0) → (x₀ + w√2 − h, h) → (x₀ − h, h) |
| Compact variant (`hazard-band-compact-*`) | w = h (P = 2√2·h), the classic tape rhythm; for thin bands off the slide (see "Standard or compact" below) |
| Vertical bands | Same geometry rotated so the stripe still reads `/` |

**Colours:** gold on white (on gold fields and light pages), gold on navy (navy fields), navy on gold
(`hazard-band-navy-gold-1920x96.*`, for gold fields where white would glare). No other pairs.

**Files and sizes** (`assets/patterns/`):

| File | Placed size |
|---|---|
| `hazard-band-gold-{white,navy}-1920x64.{svg,png}` | Slide edge: 10 × 0.333 in |
| `hazard-band-gold-{white,navy}-1920x96.{svg,png}` | Heavier slide edge: 10 × 0.5 in |
| `hazard-band-gold-{white,navy}-1920x40.{svg,png}` | Thin slide rule: 10 × 0.208 in |
| `hazard-band-gold-{white,navy}-3840x192.{svg,png}` | Banners and custom posters: 48 × 2.4 in, or scale the SVG. (The poster templates draw their own 1.25 in band at the exact page width; for a band of another height, tile `hazard-tile-gold-navy-h96.svg` at that height) |
| `hazard-band-compact-gold-{white,navy}-{1920x96,1920x64,1920x40}.{svg,png}` | w = h bands (periods 271.5 / 181.0 / 113.1 px): web section edges, print rules, sticker and merch borders |
| `hazard-tile-gold-{white,navy}-h96.{svg,png}` | 543 × 96 seamless tile for any length (repeat-x) |
| `hazard-band-vertical-gold-{white,navy}-96x1080.*` | Left or right edge bands |

**Standard or compact.** On slides, always the standard w = 2h band: it is the proportion measured from the lab's
own section slide, and the 64 px section-slide band (`hazard-band-gold-white-1920x64`) stays standard, as in
`assets/templates/ATR-Presentation-Template.pptx`. Off the slide, prefer the compact w = h band for thin bands of
64 px or less (web section edges, print rules, sticker and merch borders), where the long 2h stripes read as
slivers; this is the rule in `assets/patterns/README.md`. Never mix the two on one piece.

**How to place it**

- PowerPoint (pptxgenjs): `slide.addImage({ path: "assets/patterns/hazard-band-gold-white-1920x64.png", x: 0, y: 0, w: 10, h: 0.333 })`.
  Keep the file's aspect ratio (1920:64 = 30:1); never stretch one direction only.
- Web: CSS on an element whose height is the band height h, so it works at any page width. Stripe width, measured
  across the stripes, is h for compact (thin edges) or 2h for standard; a 32 px compact edge is
  `background: repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, #EFAB00 0 32px, #FFFFFF 32px 64px);` (135deg gives the `/`
  direction; `0 64px, #FFFFFF 64px 128px` is the standard proportion at the same height). For a standard band you
  can also tile `background: url(hazard-tile-gold-white-h96.svg) repeat-x left center / auto 100%;`. Mark it
  decorative (`aria-hidden="true"` or a CSS background).
- Print: use the SVG so the stripes stay crisp; let the band bleed off the trim edge (0.125 in bleed).
- Video and video-call backgrounds: keep stripes at least 40 px wide and the band on one edge only; fine stripes
  across a frame turn into moiré after compression.

**Where it is allowed:** section-divider top edge; title-slide bottom edge; closing-slide top edge; statement-slide
bottom edge (the navy "ATR - Statement" layout, a bookend like the title slide, at most one or two per talk); poster
header rule; event, demo and safety signage; the gold virtual background; sticker and merch borders (UCM approval
applies).
**Never:** under or behind text; as a title underline; on every slide; on white content slides or quad charts; on card or
panel edges; animated as a crawling or scrolling stripe; in any colours but the three pairs above.

## 4. Triangle lattice, chevrons, blueprint grid

**Triangle lattice** (`triangle-lattice-*`): three families of hairlines at 0°, +60° and −60°; equilateral side 96 px;
seamless tile 96 × 166 px; stroke 1.5 px at 1920 px (keep about 0.08 % of the artboard width when you rescale).
It is quiet texture for covers, heroes and title slides. Use the `navy-on-midnight`, `gold12-on-navy` and
`line-on-white` strengths only; they are safe behind large titles. Never behind body text at a stronger contrast.

**Chevron / gripper** (`chevron-*`): the gripper's 90° mitred elbow repeated as `^` in brick-offset rows. Pitch 96 px,
leg run 28 px (leg 39.6 px at 45°), row height 56 px, tile 96 × 112 px, stroke 6 px (4 px for `line-on-white`),
mitre joins, butt caps. The bold versions (`gold-on-navy`, `navy-on-white`) are accents for strips, merch and
backplates, never full-slide backgrounds behind text. The strips (`chevron-strip-*-1920x64`) are the web and print
divider.

**Blueprint grid** (`blueprint-grid-*`): minor cell 24 px, major 120 px (5 × 5), 1 px lines on the half pixel,
120 × 120 tile. Navy version: mist lines at 8 % (minor) and 18 % (major); flattened equivalents minor `#134A80`,
major `#2C5F8E`. Midnight version: sky lines at 14 % / 30 %. White version: `line` at 55 % / 100 %. It is for
technical content (system diagrams, ROS graphs, code, engineering posters), never for marketing or outreach, where
it reads as clutter. Keep it paper-like; if it starts to look like a chart grid, it is too strong.

**Accessibility:** patterns are decorative (`alt=""`, or mark as decorative in PowerPoint). Text over any pattern must
still meet 4.5:1 against the ground colour, and the pattern must stay low-contrast (see references/accessibility.md).

## 5. The mark as a supergraphic

The only sanctioned crop of the mark (references/logo-system.md, and `assets/logos/README.md` section 9): the
canonical mark, very large, cropped by the page edge, as a background device on slides, covers and banners.

- File: `assets/logos/svg/atr-mark-navy.svg` or `assets/logos/svg/atr-mark-white.svg` (PNG masters in
  `assets/logos/png/` for Google Slides).
- Colour, one per use, flat (the table and print equivalents are in references/logo-system.md §11): white at 6-10 %
  on navy; navy at 4-6 % on white or mist; solid navy `#003976` on midnight (tone on tone). Never navy on navy (it
  disappears) and never on gold.
- Keep it at least 60 % of the page height (1.2-2× the page height works well) and crop it on one or two edges; the
  roof or a gripper arm should be recognisable, not a random fragment.
- Only when a full ATR lockup is also on the same page. It is decoration, not the logo, and never replaces one.
- Never behind body text; never the roundel, badge or lockups; never gold at reduced opacity (it goes muddy); never
  together with a lattice or chevron pattern on the same surface.

## 6. Corners: square, chamfered, rounded

The mark has no curves, so ATR containers are square by default. The brand's own corner is the **45° chamfer**
(a straight cut across one corner), which echoes the roof and the gripper without copying them.

| Shape | Use for | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Square (radius 0) | Default: panels, photos, charts, tables, slide zones | `--atr-radius-none` |
| One 45° chamfer, top-right | Brand accent containers: stat callouts, feature cards, section-number plates, lower-third plates, one hero photo frame per page | Chamfer leg: slides 0.15 in, web 16 px, social 24 px at 1080 px, posters 0.75 in |
| Small radius 2 / 4 / 8 px | Web UI controls only: inputs, buttons, code blocks | `--atr-radius-sm/md/lg` in `assets/tokens/tokens.css` |
| Full pill | Status chips only (web and slides) | Text + shape + colour, never colour alone |
| Circle | Icon badges (`assets/icons/badges/`) and avatar crops only | See references/iconography.md |

How to draw the chamfer:

- python-pptx: `s = slide.shapes.add_shape(MSO_SHAPE.SNIP_1_RECTANGLE, x, y, w, h)` then
  `s.adjustments[0] = 0.15 / min(w_in, h_in)` (the value is a fraction of the shorter side; the default 0.167 is
  too big on large panels).
- pptxgenjs: `slide.addShape(pptx.shapes.SNIP_1_RECTANGLE, { x, y, w, h, fill: { color: "003976" }, rectRadius: 0.15 })`
  (pptxgenjs writes `rectRadius`, in inches, as the snip size).
- CSS: `clip-path: polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 16px) 0, 100% 16px, 100% 100%, 0 100%);`. A clip-path also clips
  borders, shadows and focus outlines, so put the focus ring on an inner element or draw the shape as an SVG
  background on focusable cards.

Rules: one chamfer per container, same size across a page, same corner (top-right) everywhere. Don't mix chamfered
and rounded containers on one surface. No large rounded cards (12 px+ radius), no blobs, no irregular polygons as
text containers (that is Kent State's "holding shapes" accent, see section 9).

## 7. Layout grids and spacing per format

Spacing tokens (web and social): the 4 px scale in `assets/tokens/layout.json`: 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64,
96, 128 px (`--atr-space-<px>`). Print and slides use the unit given per format below. Always align to the
grid, left-align text and let whitespace separate groups instead of rules and boxes.

### 16:9 slide, 10 × 5.625 in (1920 × 1080 px at 192 px/in)

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Side margins | 0.5 in (96 px), the same as the video graphics-safe area (5 %) |
| Top / bottom margins | 0.35 in |
| Columns | 12 columns, 0.567 in wide, 0.2 in gutters (content width 9.0 in) |
| Common spans | Halves 4.4 in; thirds 2.867 in; quarters 2.1 in; 8 + 4 columns = 5.933 in + 2.867 in |
| Title zone | y 0.35-1.05 in |
| Body zone | y 1.25-4.95 in |
| Footer zone | y 5.05-5.35 in (co-brand signatures, slide number) |
| Spacing unit | 0.1 in; use 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 and 0.6 in steps |
| Background clear zones | `bg-title` left 55 % (x 0-5.5 in); `bg-section` left 60 %; `bg-light` central 70 % (x 1.04-8.33 in) |

Quad charts use a tighter 0.3 in outer margin and their own zone boxes: see references/quad-charts.md. Per-layout
placeholder boxes are in references/presentations.md.

### US Letter, 8.5 × 11 in (flyers, one-pagers, handouts)

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Bleed / safe | 0.125 in bleed; nothing important within 0.25 in of the trim |
| Margins | 0.5 in all sides (flyers); letters on letterhead use Kent State's ≥ 1 in sides, 2 in top, 1.5 in bottom |
| Columns | 6 columns, 1.042 in wide, 0.25 in gutters (content width 7.5 in); halves 3.625 in, thirds 2.333 in |
| Vertical unit | 0.125 in (9 pt); space between groups 0.25 or 0.375 in |
| Hazard band | Event and safety flyers only: one full-bleed edge band, 0.25-0.375 in tall |

Tabloid (11 × 17 in): same structure with 0.625 in margins and 0.375 in gutters.

  the title block), never under text or logos. The signature row: landscape, ATR horizontal-short 7.0 in wide, the
  QR code and the Kent State wordmark in one row at the header's right; portrait, ATR horizontal-short 5.5 in wide

- **Custom posters** (another size, or no template): keep the 1.5 in margins, 1.0 in gutters and equal columns, and

- **Portrait, custom:** use the 4:1 header as is: 36 in wide gives a 9 in header (19 % of 48 in), A0 portrait

Action-safe inset 67 px (x) / 38 px (y); graphics-safe 96 px / 54 px, the same as the slide side margin. Lower thirds
live in the band y ≈ 760-900 px, left edge at x = 96, above the caption zone. Details in references/imagery.md
(shooting) and section 8 below (motion).

## 8. Motion for slides, video and web

Motion is restrained and mechanical, like a well-tuned robot: short, linear or ease-out, never bouncy. It should
help the audience follow, not decorate.

| Context | Allowed | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Slide transitions | None (default) or Fade; Wipe from left on section dividers only | 0.3-0.5 s |
| Slide builds | Appear or Fade, one bullet or one diagram step at a time, only when the talk paces it | 0.2-0.3 s |
| Hazard band on a divider | May wipe in from the left with the slide | 0.4 s, once |
| Continuity between two slides | Morph only for the same object moving (e.g. a diagram zooming into one node) | 0.5-0.8 s |
| **Chevron wipe** (video transitions, bumpers) | A 45° linear wipe whose leading edge carries a navy band with the gold chevron strip (`chevron-strip-gold-on-navy-1920x64.svg`), moving left to right | 12-15 frames at 30 fps (0.4-0.5 s), ease-in-out |
| Logo bumper (video open/close) | The lockup fades in, or is revealed by a 45° mask wipe; holds, fades out | Reveal 0.5 s, hold ≥ 1.5 s, total 2-3 s |
| Lower third | Navy plate with a top-right chamfer enters with a short 45° wipe; white text | In 8-10 frames, hold ≥ 4 s, out 6-8 frames |
| Web UI | Opacity and small translate only | 150-250 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` on enter, ease-in on exit |

Accessibility and limits:

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` on the web: switch transitions to a plain fade or none.
- Nothing flashes more than 3 times per second (WCAG 2.3.1). Robot LEDs and strobes in footage count: trim them.
- Anything that moves for more than 5 s on a web page needs a pause control (WCAG 2.2.2); avoid auto-moving
  backgrounds entirely.
- Never animate the mark's parts (no drawing-on, rebuilding, rotating, spinning, bouncing, 3D or morphing of the
  logo; references/logo-system.md lists it as misuse). Never scroll or crawl the hazard stripes.
- No PowerPoint Fly-in, Bounce, Spin, Zoom, Swivel or other entrance gimmicks.

## 9. Don'ts

| Don't | Why | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Use Kent State's brand accents (**bolts**: the gold lightning-bolt stroke and the blue angled band with a small bolt; **holding shapes**: irregular angular navy polygons used as text containers; **textures**: hand-drawn dot, wave and crosshatch patterns) as lab motifs | They are the university's devices (https://www.kent.edu/brand/accents) and belong on pieces where Kent State leads | Use the lab's hazard band, lattice, chevrons and chamfer |
| Use the Kent State **sunburst** or seal as a graphic element, or crop it from the wordmark | UCM must approve any sunburst use (https://www.kent.edu/ucm/kent-state-university-sunburst); the seal is reserved for university officials | Keep the Kent State wordmark whole, as a separate signature (references/kent-state-compliance.md) |
| Use athletics marks (the Flash, the K/eagle) | "For the use of Kent State athletics only" | The academic KENT STATE UNIVERSITY wordmark |
| Put text on a hazard band, or use it as a title underline | High-contrast stripes wreck legibility; underlines under titles are an AI-slide tell | Band on the page edge; titles stand alone |
| Add stripes or colour bars to card edges | A generic template tell | The top-right chamfer, or nothing |
| Stack devices (band + lattice + chevrons + supergraphic) | Noise; the piece looks like a pattern catalogue | One pattern and one band per surface at most |
| Put patterns over photos | Texture fights the image | A navy scrim for text (references/imagery.md) |
| Use gradients, glows, drop shadows, bevels, 3D, lens flares | The brand is flat, precise geometry | Flat token colours. Exactly two gradients are sanctioned (references/color.md §2): a graded navy scrim over a photo, and navy to midnight on a large navy field (web hero, banner, cover) with no small text across it. Never into gold, sky or black, never on type, icons or logos |
| Draw at free angles, add curves or round joins | Breaks the 45°/60° system | 0°, 45°, 90°, and 60° for lattices only |
| Use cream, beige or warm-gray backgrounds | Off-palette and a generic template tell | White or mist `#F3F6FA` |
| Recolour patterns (sky stripes, flash-yellow bands) | Weakens the gold/navy equity | The token pairs in the pattern files |
| Stretch pattern PNGs or bands in one direction | Distorts the 45° angle | Scale proportionally; tile or use the SVG for other lengths |
| Use generic tech clichés (circuit-board traces, binary rain, glowing brains, blue robot hands) | They say nothing about this lab | Real lab photos (references/imagery.md) or the lab's own geometry |
| Hand-edit the pattern PNGs | They are generated; edits drift from the geometry | Regenerate with `build/illustrations-src/patterns.py` in the source repo, or draw from the SVG |
