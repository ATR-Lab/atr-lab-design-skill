# ATR brand patterns and graphic devices

Deterministic vector patterns derived from the ATR mark's geometry (straight strokes, 45° angles,
flat bars, no curves), plus a 60° triangle lattice as a companion texture. Every file here is generated by
`build/illustrations-src/patterns.py` (Python, writes SVG; PNGs rasterized with `rsvg-convert`).
Re-run that script to regenerate everything; do not hand-edit the PNGs.

Colors are the brand tokens exactly (see `assets/tokens/`): navy `#003976`, gold `#EFAB00`,
midnight `#00295F`, sky `#2C8ECD`, mist `#F3F6FA`, line `#D6DEE8`, white `#FFFFFF`.

| Pattern | Use it for | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| Hazard-stripe band | Section dividers, title/closing slide edge, poster header rule, the gold virtual background, event signage | Do not put it on every slide; never as a text underline; never in colors other than gold/white, gold/navy, navy/gold |
| Triangle lattice | Full-bleed subtle background texture behind titles, web hero sections, print covers | Never behind body text at the navy-on-midnight strength; keep contrast low (it is texture, not content) |
| Chevron / gripper | Accent strips, dividers, merch, web section separators, icon backplates | Not as a full-slide background behind text (too busy at the 6 px stroke); use the `line-on-white` or `navy-on-midnight` version if it must sit behind content |
| Blueprint grid | Technical/system-architecture slides, diagrams, code or ROS-topic slides, engineering posters | Not on marketing or outreach pieces; keep opacity low so it reads as paper, not as a chart grid |

## 1. Hazard-stripe band (`hazard-*`)

The lab's signature motif, measured from the original section-slide artwork
(`ATR Presentation Template.pptx`, media `image7.jpg`): a square frame of diagonal gold/white stripes.

Construction (all values scale with the band height **h**):

- Angle: **45°**, stripes rise to the right (`/`); as y increases, x decreases by the same amount.
- Stripe width, measured perpendicular to the stripe: **w = 2h** (measured ratio in the original:
  stripe ≈ 159 px on a 78 px band ⇒ 2.04). Foreground and ground are equal (**1:1**).
- Horizontal period **P = 2·w·√2 = 4√2·h ≈ 5.657h** (1920×96 band ⇒ P = 543.06 px, each gold
  parallelogram runs 271.5 px along the band).
- Each stripe is a parallelogram with vertices (x₀,0) (x₀+w√2,0) (x₀+w√2−h,h) (x₀−h,h).
- Compact variant (`hazard-band-compact-*`): **w = h** (classic tape proportion, P = 2√2·h) for
  thin bands (≤ 64 px) **off the slide** (web, print, merch) where the 2h stripe would look like long
  slivers; shipped at 96, 64 and 40 px. **Slides always use the standard w = 2h band**, including the
  64 px section-slide band: that is the proportion measured from the lab's own section slide.
- Vertical bands use the same geometry rotated so the stripe still reads as `/`.

PPTX use: insert the standard (not compact) PNG as a picture, full slide width. Section slides
(`hazard-band-gold-white-1920x64`) and closing slides (`hazard-band-gold-navy-1920x64`) put it on the
**top edge** at y = 0; on the closing slide a bottom band would clip the line-work cluster in the
bottom-right of `bg-closing-16x9.png`. The optional title-slide band (`hazard-band-gold-navy-1920x64`)
sits on the bottom edge. In pptxgenjs:
`slide.addImage({path: "assets/patterns/hazard-band-gold-white-1920x64.png", x:0, y:0, w:10, h:0.333})`
(0.333 in = 64 px at 192 px/in on a 10 in slide; the 1920x96 file is 0.5 in). Never stretch it
non-uniformly. Placement per slide type: `references/graphic-elements.md` §2.

## 2. Triangle lattice (`triangle-lattice-*`)

Three families of hairlines at 0°, +60°, −60°. This is a companion motif, not a copy of the roof: the
roof of the mark is a right isosceles triangle (45°/45°/90°), not an equilateral one. Never "correct"
the roof to 60° to match the lattice.

- Equilateral side **s = 96 px**; tile = **s × round(s√3)** = 96 × 166 px (two rows of triangles;
  the 0.28 px rounding makes the diagonals 59.96° instead of 60° and lets the PNG tile pixel-exactly).
- Lines in the tile: horizontals at y = 0 and y = h/2; +60° lines (0,0)→(s,h), (s/2,0)→(s,h/2),
  (0,h/2)→(s/2,h); −60° lines mirrored. Segments overshoot the tile by 0.75 px so butt caps never
  open a seam.
- Stroke **1.5 px** (at 1920 px width; keep ≈ 0.08% of the artboard width when rescaling).

| File | Colors | Intended surface |
|---|---|---|
| `triangle-lattice-tile-navy-on-midnight.svg/.png`, `triangle-lattice-navy-on-midnight-1920x1080.*` | navy lines on midnight | dark title/closing/web hero backgrounds |
| `triangle-lattice-tile-line-on-white.*`, `triangle-lattice-line-on-white-1920x1080.*` | line `#D6DEE8` on white | light content backgrounds, print covers |
| `triangle-lattice-tile-gold12-on-navy.*`, `triangle-lattice-gold12-on-navy-1920x1080.*` | gold at 12% opacity on navy | behind the mark on navy title slides |

## 3. Chevron / gripper pattern (`chevron-*`)

The mark's gripper arm is a straight bar with a **90° mitred elbow** (both legs at 45°). The
pattern repeats that elbow as a `^` chevron in brick-offset rows.

- Pitch **P = 96 px**; leg horizontal run **28 px** (leg length 28√2 = 39.6 px); rise 28 px.
- Row height 56 px; tile = **96 × 112 px** (two rows, second row offset by P/2).
- Stroke **6 px** (bold, gold-on-navy / navy-on-white / navy-on-midnight) or **4 px** (line-on-white).
- `stroke-linejoin: miter` (sharp apex, like the mark), `stroke-linecap: butt`.

## 4. Blueprint grid (`blueprint-grid-*`)

Fine drafting grid for technical slides.

- Minor cell **24 px**, major cell **120 px** (5 × 5 minors); tile = 120 × 120 px; stroke 1 px,
  lines drawn on the half-pixel (x + 0.5) so they stay crisp at 1:1.
- `blueprint-grid-navy-1920x1080`: mist `#F3F6FA` lines at **8%** (minor) / **18%** (major) opacity on navy.
- `blueprint-grid-midnight-1920x1080`: sky `#2C8ECD` lines at 14% / 30% on midnight (more "blueprint").
- `blueprint-grid-white-1920x1080`: line `#D6DEE8` at 55% / 100% on white (for light technical slides).
- `blueprint-grid-tile-navy`, `blueprint-grid-tile-white`: 120 × 120 seamless tiles.

Effective solid colors if you need to reproduce the navy grid without transparency:
minor ≈ `#134A80`, major ≈ `#2C5F8E` (mist over navy at 8% and 18%).

## Accessibility notes

- Patterns are decoration: text placed over them must still meet 4.5:1 against the **ground**
  color, and the pattern must be low-contrast enough not to interfere (the `navy-on-midnight`,
  `gold12-on-navy`, `line-on-white` variants are safe behind large titles; the bold gold chevron
  is not).
- Hazard bands are high-contrast by design and should never sit under text.
- Give the pattern picture no alt text (decorative, `alt=""`) in slides and on the web.
