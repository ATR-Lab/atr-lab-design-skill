Until each is answered, the skill uses a placeholder or the safer reading.

**For the lab director**

1. Should "Lab" or "Laboratory" be the default long form? The skill defaults to "Lab".
2. Which of the two LinkedIn pages is canonical (the other carries the retired logo)? Is facebook.com/ATRLabatKSU the
   lab's page?
3. What are the lab's own room number and phone line? What kent.edu address should replace the Gmail address on K-12,
   recruiting and sponsor pieces?
4. Should the lab adopt a tagline? None is official; references/brand-foundation.md §4 lists the lines in use and three drafts.
5. Do you approve the draft positioning statement, one-liner, 25-, 50- and 100-word boilerplates and social bio
   (references/brand-foundation.md §4, §10)?
6. Are the team names ATR_Kent, ATR_FLUX, ATR Pepper Team and ATR_ENT still active? Did the ATR_Kent team attend
   RoboCup 2023 in Bordeaux?
7. Please confirm three proof-bank details:
   - the exact name, paper and recipients of the 2021 World Robot Summit Distinguished Paper Award
   - the lab's current role in the NSF PRIDe project
   - whether the 2020 NASA SUITS team traveled to Johnson Space Center
8. Which award funded which work? Grant numbers stay `[Grant No.]` until you confirm them.
9. What is the current sponsor tier table? The Donate page and the RoboCup 2023 page conflict. Confirm it together with
   the Division of Philanthropy and Alumni Engagement.
10. Are the stacked lockups (compositions of the lab's own pieces) official? Can the original designer supply vector
    masters of the 2021 lockup and the stacked artwork, for large prints?

**For Kent State University Communications and Marketing (UCM)**

1. May research labs keep their own marks? Will UCM review and approve the ATR mark, the roundel and the
   separate-signature co-branding layout?
2. Does a College of Sciences and Humanities logo or a Department of Computer Science logo exist? Please supply the
   official Kent State Horizontal and Stacked vector files (and an All Blue version). The skill ships only a
   working-copy raster.
3. Is there a digital minimum size in pixels for the Kent State logo, or a size ratio between a unit mark and the Kent
   State logo?
4. Kent State logo on quad charts: the legacy rule puts the logo on everything, but it cannot fit a quad header at its
   minimum. May the lab name Kent State in text instead, as the template does?
5. Does "all forms of visual communications" cover plain photo or video posts, social avatars and video-call virtual
   backgrounds? The lab currently leaves the logo off these.
6. Who owns the ATR mark, and is it registered? This is for the Office of General Counsel. The skill assumes a
   KSU-owned, unregistered mark: ™ at most, never ®.
7. What is the licensing path for merch? Does the vendor submit the design? Are in-house fabrication, cut vinyl for lab
   equipment and departmental poster plotters exempt from the contracted-vendor rule?
8. Is the lab website (atr.cs.kent.edu, WordPress) exempt from Policy 9-02.3, and does it need microsite approval?
   Which analytics may it use? This is for the Web Team.
9. Do hashtags the lab coins (the proposed #ATRKent) need UCM sign-off, or only a courtesy check?
10. What is the current equal-opportunity statement for recruiting pieces?
11. What is in the UCM PowerPoint portal, which needs a KSU login?
12. Will UCM accept "ATR Lab" as a second reference in its own publications?
13. Is "Mathematical Sciences Building" (the campus list and the department footer) the right building name? The
    style guide still shows "Mathematics and Computer Science Building".
14. What lead time does UCM prefer for embargoed research news, and will it apply its own house style to lab releases?
15. Which bulk-email tool should the lab use? The Division of Information Technology may be the one to ask.
